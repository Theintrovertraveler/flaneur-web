The fine print,
said plainly.

Flâneur plans your day with real care, but the world moves. Here is what we cannot promise, stated without small print games.

1. Opening hours and seasonal changes

Every itinerary is calculated using the opening hours in effect at the moment it is generated. Those hours can change without notice. This matters most around the switch between summer and winter schedules, when a museum may open later or close earlier than the plan assumes, and this can shift the result. Always check the venue's official hours before you set out.

2. Artworks and what is on display

A work named in your itinerary may not be on view when you arrive. Pieces are moved for restoration, lent to other institutions, pulled into temporary exhibitions, or shifted during rehangs. We cannot guarantee that any specific work is physically present on your visit.

3. Accuracy of information

Our data is built and maintained with editorial rigour, but it is not infallible. Errors and omissions are possible. If you notice something wrong, tell us through the feedback form and we will look into it.

4. Bookings, tickets, and prices

Availability, prices, and booking requirements are set by the venues and their ticketing partners, and they change. Treat any booking link or price in your itinerary as a starting point, and confirm the current terms on the official channel before you pay.

5. Distances and travel times

Walking distances and travel times are estimates. Real conditions such as traffic, transport disruptions, closures, weather, and your own pace will vary, and so will the time each leg actually takes.

6. Restaurant suggestions

Food recommendations are editorial. Some places have been visited and written about first hand; others are chosen through cross-referenced research and local input. In both cases hours, menus, and availability can change, and a suggestion is never a reservation.

7. Affiliate links

Some links in the itinerary are affiliate links. If you book through one, Flâneur may earn a commission. This comes at no extra cost to you, and it never influences which places or works we select. Our editorial choices are made on their own merits.

8. Nature of the service

Flâneur is a planning tool, not a travel agency or a guarantee of any outcome. The final decisions about your trip, and the responsibility for them, remain yours. We provide the plan; you choose how to use it.
